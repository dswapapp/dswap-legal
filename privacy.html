Privacy Policy

Operator: Dswap. Dswap stores doll-box records, drafts, favorites, swap chats, reports, blocked accounts, and coin balance locally on your device.

Camera and Photos are used only when you add doll or reference photos. Microphone is used only when you record a voice note in chat. Push notifications may alert you about swap status updates you opt into on this device.

In-app purchases grant consumable Sparks. We do not sell personal data. Log Out keeps local data; Delete Account erases local data and remaining Sparks permanently.
