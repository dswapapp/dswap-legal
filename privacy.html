01 Introduction

Who we are & how to read this

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dswap mobile application (“Dswap”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the app to browse listings, manage your Doll Box, chat about swaps, unlock themes or card styles with Sparks, and keep community safety tools nearby.

Dswap is designed as a local-first experience. Most of what you create—doll records, drafts, chats, favorites, reports, and Spark balances—lives in on-device storage controlled by your phone. We do not operate a separate Dswap cloud profile that syncs your cabinet across strangers’ devices.

Plain language If it is on your screen in Dswap, it is usually sitting in local storage on your iPhone—not in a Dswap marketing database.
02 Local storage

What we store on your device

Depending on how you use Dswap, the app may keep the following categories locally:

  • Identity on device — display name, handle, bio, and optional profile photo you set in Me.
  • Doll Box — seat layouts, catalog dolls you place, custom photos (including optional cutout previews), backgrounds, and cabinet themes you unlock.
  • Market activity — drafts, published listings or looking-for posts, favorites, and search/filter preferences for this install.
  • Messages — chat threads, pinned swap cards, voice-note files you record, and swap status notes for peers you talk with.
  • Safety tools — reports you submit and accounts you block or mute on this device.
  • Sparks ledger — local coin balance, welcome grant flag, and unlock flags for themes, card templates, capacity expansions, and paid placements.
  • Preferences — legal-agree flag, login session flag, notification permission state handled by iOS, and similar app settings.

Photos you pick for listings, avatars, chat attachments, or Doll Box placement may be compressed and stored as image or audio files in the app’s sandboxed Documents / local store. They are used only to show your content back to you inside Dswap.

03 Permissions

When iOS asks for access

Dswap requests system permissions only when you tap a control that needs them—not on splash or Enter. You can keep typing or skip a feature if you decline.

Camera

When you tap Take Photo on publish, profile, chat attach, or Doll Box add-photo flows, so you can capture doll pictures for listings, avatars, or cabinet placement.

Photos

When you tap Photo Library in those same scenes, to import doll or reference images you already own.

Microphone

When you tap the mic in chat to record a short voice note about a swap. You can always type instead.

Notifications

iOS may show the system Push alert early so Dswap can optionally remind you about swap status on this device. You can keep notifications off.

No tracking transparency Dswap does not use App Tracking Transparency, IDFA, or cross-app advertising identifiers.
04 Purchases

Sparks & Apple billing

Consumable Sparks (in-app currency) can unlock cabinet expansions, Theme Studio packs, chat card styles, and paid Doll Box placements. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple receives the payment information needed to complete the transaction under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Dswap stores the resulting Spark balance and unlock state locally so the app can show what you own. We do not sell personal data. Spending Sparks always shows a confirmation dialog first.

If a purchase fails, is cancelled, or cannot be verified, Sparks are not granted. Welcome Sparks, if offered, are a one-time local grant on this install—not an Apple product.

05 Community

Chat, listings & safety signals

When you post listings, send messages, attach photos, or record voice notes, that content stays in your local threads and feeds for this install. Safety features (report, block, content filters, community guidelines) keep signals on device so you can hide people or content that break salon norms.

Dswap may apply on-device filters to blocked words before a message is saved. Filtered text is not uploaded to a Dswap server for moderation analytics in this local-first build.

06 Limits

What we intentionally skip

  • No sale of personal information to data brokers
  • No third-party advertising SDKs wired for behavioral ads
  • No ATT / IDFA collection for tracking across other companies’ apps
  • No requirement to create a cloud password account to browse the salon

Apple, iOS, and the App Store may process diagnostics or purchase records according to your Apple ID settings. That processing is outside Dswap’s local ledger.

07 Children

Age-appropriate use

Dswap is meant for doll collectors and swap negotiation. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to share sexual content involving minors. If you believe a minor’s data was stored on a shared device, use Delete Account on that install and contact us.

08 Retention

Log Out, Delete Account & backups

Log Out

Returns you to Enter. Local doll data, chats, and Sparks generally remain on the device unless you delete the app or wipe the account.

Delete Account

Erases local profile content, cabinet seats, threads, drafts, unlocks, and remaining Sparks for this install after you confirm. This cannot be undone.

Uninstalling Dswap removes the app sandbox, including local files. iCloud or computer backups of your phone may still hold older copies until those backups rotate—managed by Apple / your backup settings, not by a Dswap server.

09 Updates

Changes to this policy

We may refine this Privacy Policy as features grow (for example, new permission scenes or purchase types). The “Updated” chip at the top shows the latest revision date. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy inside the app reader.

10 Contact

Talk to Dswap

Questions about privacy, local data, or Delete Account outcomes can be sent through in-app Support / Contact, or emailed to support@dswap.app. Please describe the device and roughly when the issue happened so we can help within the limits of a local-first app.
